## Notes

- Patient-level split: 0 shared patients between train and val.
- Talk wording: “about 0.94 val AUROC,” not 0.9363.
- Accuracy is the trap: always-benign is 97.6% accurate; the model at the high-sensitivity cutoff is only 87% accurate and is still the better ranker (AUROC 0.94 vs 0.50).
- At that cutoff we catch 76/94 cancers and miss 18, at the cost of 465 false alarms. Precision is 14%: most flags are still benign.
- pAUC 0.193 is on *this* 20k-benign val split, not the hidden ISIC test set. Do not compare it to Kaggle leaderboard 0.17–0.18 as if we won. The slide point is “pAUC is the high-sensitivity slice, and it is a different (harder) question than AUROC.”
